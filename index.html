DAYS 0 – 10

Warning

The charge failed. We tell them calmly and give them a button to update their card.

  • Amber banner, always visible
  • Pop-up once a day
  • Emails on days 0, 3 and 7
DAYS 11 – 13

Final notice

Only a few days left. Everything turns red and we explain what will stop working.

  • Red banner; on the last day it says “tomorrow”
  • Pop-up on every visit
  • Emails on days 11 and 13
DAY 14 ONWARDS

Account suspended

Online check-in, police registration and guest payments stop. Their data stays saved.

  • One email on day 14 and another on day 21
  • Both lead to “Reactivate my account”
ANY TIME

The customer pays

The banner, the pop-up and every email lead to the same screen: they add a new card and we charge it right away.

  • If the bank declines it, we tell them why
  • If it goes through, they’re back on the dashboard with no warnings
  • They get the E8 confirmation email
Users without Billing access

What a team member sees

They see the same warning, but without the amount, the card or a pay button. We tell them who on their team can fix it.
